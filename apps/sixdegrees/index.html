FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

Is my data stored on your servers?

No. Data lives on your device and optionally in your own GitHub repository.

Do I need GitHub?

No. The app works offline without GitHub. Sync is optional.

Can I import my contacts?

Yes. Import from your phonebook and avoid duplicates.

Which platforms are supported?

Android today. iOS is built with the same codebase.
